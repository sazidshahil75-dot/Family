iPhone: turn location on for Safari
  1. Tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings, then Location, then Allow.
  2. If that is missing: Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Safari Websites, choose While Using the App.
  3. Come back here, reload the page and tap Allow location. On Android Chrome: tap the lock icon, then Permissions, then Location.
